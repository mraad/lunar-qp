Quadratic MPC · Lunar landing

At each observation, a quadratic program chooses a short plan of engine-duty fractions. Only the first fraction is converted into one discrete engine command. The controller then observes, updates its physics estimate, and solves again. This is a recorded replay, not an optimization running in the browser.

QP minimizes squared tracking errors subject to linearized dynamics and engine limits. The dashed path assumes fractional duties; the actual lander executes individual engine pulses. Soft approach limits and pulse allocation do not guarantee a safe or integer-optimal landing.

Solid teal: actual path · Dashed amber: predicted future · flags: landing pad.
