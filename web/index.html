A solved QP is a plan—not a landing guarantee.

This app replays recorded Python/OSQP decisions. It does not run new optimization in the browser. The gold path uses fractional thrust; the real lander uses discrete engine pulses. Small prediction errors can accumulate even when the QP solves successfully.

Slack relaxes an approach margin at a cost. Positive slack does not certify safe terrain clearance. Residual measures numerical constraint violation. A low residual says the mathematical plan is feasible to tolerance, not that its physics is exact.

Adaptive QP updates engine estimates during flight. Fixed QP still replans, but retains its initial model. Both use the same four discrete actions. The 30% fault is introduced by the evaluator and never disclosed to either controller.
